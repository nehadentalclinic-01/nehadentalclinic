About the clinic

A dental practice built on trust, across every branch.

Neha Dental Clinic, Orthodontic & Implant Centre treats families across its branches, combining experienced diagnosis with a gentle, modern approach to general and cosmetic dentistry.

  • Experienced team in general and cosmetic dentistry
